Clearinghouse · Setup

Claims don't go straight from your office to an insurer. They pass through a clearinghouse — a switchboard that checks each claim's format, forwards it to the right payer, and returns the payer's answer. This wizard takes you from nothing to a live connection. It saves as you go, so you can close it and come back; enrollment runs for weeks, and nothing here forgets where you were.
