AI Pre-flight review

A denial-prevention second opinion on a claim before you submit it. Runs the same clean-claim scrub the submit endpoint runs, then an AI reviewer predicts the denials a payer would issue — medical-necessity mismatches, NCCI bundling, missing modifiers — each tagged with the CARC it would draw, so you fix it first. No patient identity is sent to the model; only codes.

Open from a claim with ?claim_id=… to prefill and auto-run.
