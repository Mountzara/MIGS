Good Faith Estimates

Required by the No Surprises Act for every uninsured or self-pay patient (45 CFR 149.610). The deadline depends on how far out the service is from the day it was scheduled, so it is computed for you here. Items furnished by an outside lab, imaging centre or specialist are listed separately because they bill the patient directly — and a patient billed $400 or more above this estimate can open a federal dispute.
