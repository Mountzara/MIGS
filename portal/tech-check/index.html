Test your device.

A quick check of your camera, microphone, speaker, and internet connection so your visit runs smoothly. This takes about 60 seconds.

1
Camera

We'll ask for permission to use your camera and show you a preview.

2
Microphone

We'll ask for permission to use your microphone and listen for sound.

3
Speakers / headphones

Click "Play tone" and tap "I hear it" if you hear a short chime.

4
Internet connection

We'll measure your connection speed. Telehealth needs at least 600 kbps.
