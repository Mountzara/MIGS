# GET YOUR SITE LIVE NOW

## The Problem
Your landing page files are ready, but they're sitting in GitHub - not deployed to mountzara.com yet.

## The Solution (Choose ONE)

---

### OPTION A: I Deploy It For You (Fastest)

**Give me this ONE thing and I'll handle everything:**

Go here: https://dash.cloudflare.com/profile/api-tokens/create

1. Click the blue "Create Custom Token" button
2. Give it any name (like "deploy")
3. Under Permissions, select:
   - **Account** | **Cloudflare Pages** | **Edit**
4. Click "Continue to summary"
5. Click "Create Token"
6. Copy the token it shows you
7. Paste it here in chat

**That's it. I'll deploy everything in 30 seconds.**

---

### OPTION B: You Do It (3 clicks, no token needed)

1. Go to: https://dash.cloudflare.com/
2. Click: **"Workers & Pages"** (left sidebar)
3. Click: **"Create application"**
4. Click: **"Pages"**
5. Click: **"Connect to Git"**
6. Select your **MIGS** repository
7. Click: **"Begin setup"**
8. Leave everything as default and click: **"Save and Deploy"**
9. Wait 2 minutes
10. Click: **"Custom domains"** > **"Set up a custom domain"**
11. Type: **mountzara.com**
12. Click: **"Continue"**

**Done. Site is live.**
