1. Two documents, two scopes

This Privacy Policy covers the website: what happens when you browse mountzara.com, join the waitlist, or create a member-portal account. Your health information — everything you tell us as a patient — is protected health information under HIPAA and is governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices, which describes stronger, legally required protections. Where the two overlap, the Notice of Privacy Practices controls.

2. What this website collects

When you…We collect
Browse the siteStandard technical information handled by our hosting provider (Cloudflare): IP address, browser type, pages requested. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free and does not track you across sites.
Join the membership waitlistYour email address, the state you tell us, the tier you are interested in, and whether you already have an OB/GYN. The form tells you not to include symptoms or medical history, and the server screens for and refuses clinical content — the waitlist is a mailing list, not a medical record.
Create a portal accountYour name, email, phone, and date of birth. Once you use the portal as a patient, what you enter there is PHI under the Notice of Privacy Practices.

3. How it is used

  • To operate the site and portal: signing you in, showing you your own information, and sending the service emails described below.
  • To understand interest in the membership programme, in aggregate (for example, how many signups come from outside Illinois and California).
  • To keep the site secure and diagnose problems.

4. What is never done with it

  • We do not sell your information. We never have and never will.
  • We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or social-media platforms, and this site carries no advertising trackers or third-party ad pixels.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf do so under contract, and providers that touch health information do so under HIPAA business associate agreements — our telehealth video platform (Doxy.me), our email provider (Amazon Web Services), and our hosting infrastructure.
  • We disclose information when the law genuinely requires it, and the Notice of Privacy Practices describes the limits we apply when it does.

5. Cookies and analytics

The portal uses a session cookie so you stay signed in; it identifies your session, expires, and does nothing else. The public site sets no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Our analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) measures page views without cookies and without building a profile of you.

Do Not Track: because we do not track visitors across other websites or over time for advertising, there is nothing for a browser "Do Not Track" signal to turn off — the behaviour it requests is already how this site works for everyone. We therefore treat all visitors the same whether or not the signal is present. No third party collects personal information about your activity across other sites through this one.

6. Email

Every email we send is transactional — triggered by something you did: a sign-in link you requested, an appointment you booked, a message waiting for you. We send no marketing email and no newsletter. Emails deliberately contain no clinical content; they tell you something is waiting and link you to the secured portal to read it. If your address hard-bounces or you mark a message as spam, we stop sending to it.

7. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS). Clinical records, messages, and documents are encrypted at rest with per-record keys. Access to systems is limited, authenticated, and logged. No system is perfectly secure, and if a breach affecting your information ever occurs we will notify you as HIPAA, the Illinois Personal Information Protection Act, and California Civil Code §1798.82 require.

8. Illinois and California

Illinois: we comply with the Illinois Personal Information Protection Act, including its breach-notification requirements. We do not collect biometric identifiers, so the Biometric Information Privacy Act does not apply to this site.

California: medical information about California patients is protected by the Confidentiality of Medical Information Act (CMIA) in addition to HIPAA. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as the CCPA/CPRA defines those terms; although a practice of this size is generally below that law's thresholds, we honour access and deletion requests from any patient regardless, subject to medical-record retention laws.

9. Children

The website and portal are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them online. Care for minors, where clinically appropriate, is arranged through a parent or guardian and governed by the applicable consent laws, not by a self-created web account.

10. Retention

Waitlist entries are kept until the programme launches or you ask to be removed. Portal accounts persist while active. Medical records are retained for the periods Illinois and California law require of physicians, which is measured in years — deleting a web account does not, and legally cannot, delete the medical record behind it.

11. Your choices

  • Ask to be removed from the waitlist at any time: info@mountzara.com.
  • Change your account email or close your account, which stops all email.
  • For health-information rights — access, amendment, an accounting of disclosures — see the Notice of Privacy Practices, which lists them fully.

12. Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, for changes affecting portal members, say so in the portal. Questions: info@mountzara.com or (872) 365‑3866 — Mount Zara, LLC, Chicago, Illinois.
