# Mount Zara — Malpractice Carrier Confirmation Letter (R13 template)

**Purpose:** obtain WRITTEN confirmation from the malpractice carrier that the
current policy covers Mount Zara's telehealth + e-Rx + AI-augmented modalities
in every state where Dr. Mabini holds an active license (Illinois + California
per `docs/compliance/licensure.md` §1, confirmed 2026-06-09).
**Workflow (Implementation Specs §B.4 / R13):** copy the letter below,
customize the bracketed fields, send to the carrier's underwriting department,
follow up at 14 business days, and file the carrier's written response in
`docs/compliance/malpractice-coverage-2026.md`. Set a calendar reminder for
30 days before annual policy renewal to re-confirm.

---

## Letter template

> [Date]
>
> [Carrier Name]
> Attn: Underwriting Department
> [Carrier Address]
>
> Re: Policy [policy number] — Confirmation of telehealth and multi-state coverage
>
> To whom it may concern,
>
> I am writing to confirm coverage scope for my professional liability policy
> with [Carrier]. My practice operates as a solo Complex Benign Gynecology /
> Minimally Invasive Gynecologic Surgery (CBG/MIGS) practice under the name
> Mount Zara, and includes the following modalities of care that I want to
> confirm are fully covered under my current policy:
>
> 1. Synchronous telehealth visits conducted via Doxy.me video.
> 2. Asynchronous secure messaging conducted via my practice's HIPAA-compliant
>    patient portal at mountzara.com.
> 3. Electronic prescribing including controlled substances via DoseSpot, with
>    EPCS DEA registration.
> 4. AI-augmented decision support tools (specifically the patient-snapshot
>    generator that synthesizes clinical notes from the visit transcript).
>
> I hold active medical licenses in Illinois and California, and patients
> reside in or temporarily travel through these states.
>
> Please confirm in writing that my current policy covers all four modalities
> listed above in all listed states, including extended coverage for
> established patients temporarily in non-licensed states under permitted
> circumstances.
>
> If any of these modalities is not covered, please indicate which and the
> available options for adding coverage.
>
> Thank you for your prompt attention. I am happy to schedule a call to
> discuss.
>
> Sincerely,
>
> Chris Mabini, DO, FMIGS
> Mount Zara
> info@mountzara.com

---

## Operational checklist

- [ ] Fill bracketed fields (carrier, address, policy number, date).
- [ ] Send to the carrier (email or certified mail; keep proof of sending).
- [ ] Calendar: follow-up at 14 business days if no response; escalate to a supervisor.
- [ ] File the written response in `docs/compliance/malpractice-coverage-2026.md`.
- [ ] Calendar: re-confirm 30 days before each annual policy renewal.
- [ ] If the carrier's scope is narrower than the four modalities: surface the gap immediately; either change carriers or amend practice operations to fit the available coverage.
