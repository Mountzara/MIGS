Thank you for the re-evaluation path. The four items you asked for are below, in order. The frame for all of them: this is a single-physician medical practice sending only transactional account emails, each triggered by the recipient's own action, at tens of emails per month. No marketing mail, no newsletter, no bulk sending, no mailing list -- the software cannot send to more than one recipient at a time. We do not need a high limit; we only need to send to addresses that are not pre-verified identities, which the sandbox prevents.

1. Estimated mail sending volume and frequency

- At launch: roughly 30-60 emails per month total (1-2 per day), rising to perhaps 200-400 per month at full patient panel.
- Per recipient: a patient receives an email only when they act (request a sign-in link, book an appointment) or when something they initiated completes (intake reviewed, message reply). A typical patient receives 2-5 emails around a visit and none in between.
- We are not requesting a large quota; any quota appropriate for this volume is acceptable.

2. Mailing list management procedures

There is no mailing list, by design.

- Every address enters exactly one way: the patient types their own address when creating their own portal account, to receive their own sign-in link. Sign-in is by emailed one-time link, so a working address the patient controls is a precondition of using the service.
- No purchased, rented, imported, scraped, or third-party addresses, and no mechanism to add an address except account creation by its owner.
- Every send is keyed to an existing patient record; an address without an account cannot be mailed.
- Changing the address or closing the account stops all mail immediately.

3. Process for handling bounces, complaints, and unsubscribe requests

Implemented in the application and deployed:

- A dedicated HTTPS feedback endpoint is live today (https://mountzara.com/api/v1/internal/ses/feedback). It receives SES Bounce/Complaint/Delivery notifications via Amazon SNS, validates the topic ARN, auto-confirms the subscription, and maintains a suppression list in our database. Verifying this end to end is a blocking pre-production item: no production mail is sent before the bounce/complaint pipeline is confirmed working.
- Hard bounce: suppressed immediately and permanently. Complaint: suppressed immediately and permanently -- if a recipient marks our mail as spam we stop mailing them.
- Soft bounce: recorded, not yet suppressed (a full mailbox recovers); five consecutive soft bounces escalate to suppression; a successful delivery clears the count.
- The suppression list is checked before every send, so a suppressed address is never handed to SES.
- We monitor against your published thresholds (bounce under 5%, complaint under 0.1%); at our volume every single bounce is investigated individually.
- Per your best-practices guidance on standard aliases, the application refuses to send to role addresses (postmaster@, abuse@, noc@, etc.) at the transport layer.
- Unsubscribe: every message is a service notification the recipient's own activity triggered, so there is no subscription to manage -- but the controls exist regardless: closing the account, changing the address, or asking us to stop ends all mail, and an administrator can suppress any address manually.
