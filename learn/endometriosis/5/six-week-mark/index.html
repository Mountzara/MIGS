The six-week visit: pathology, suppression, PT restart

The surgery is behind you, and now there's a quiet stretch of calendar between you and the follow-up visit. You keep adding to the list of things you want to ask before you forget them.

6 weeks and beyond

Post-op visit, pathology review on every specimen, and a decision about hormonal suppression to reduce recurrence (the national guideline explicitly notes high recurrence rates without suppression). Pelvic floor PT typically begins at 4–6 weeks. Fertility planning restarts here when relevant.

Post-operative plan: hormonal suppression resumed within 2 weeks (LNG-IUD placed intra-operatively in most cases) to reduce recurrence[6]Endometriosis recurrence following post-operative hormonal suppression: a systematic review and meta-analysis.Hum Reprod Update 2021 · PMID 33020832Zakhari et al., Hum Reprod Update — post-operative hormonal suppression significantly reduces endometriosis and pain recurrence after excision.. Pelvic-floor physical therapy started or resumed when wound-healing permits. OMT visceral and structural protocol re-introduced at the 6-week post-op mark per the Alboni protocol[11]Effects of osteopathic manipulative therapy on recurrent pelvic pain and dyspareunia in women after surgery for endometriosis: a retrospective study.Minerva Obstet Gynecol 2024 · PMID 37997320Alboni et al., Minerva Obstet Gynecol 2024 — osteopathic visceral-fascial protocol reduced recurrent pelvic pain and dyspareunia after endometriosis surgery..
