The first weeks: what's normal, what's allowed, and when you'll actually feel better

You're home on the couch on day three, cataloguing every twinge and wondering whether it's the good kind of sore or the bad kind. Someone texts 'feeling better yet?' and you don't know what to answer.

First 2 weeks

Soreness rather than sharp pain. Alternating ibuprofen and acetaminophen handles most postoperative pain. Walking begins day one. No heavy lifting (>10 lb), no intercourse, no tampons for 2 weeks. Mild CO₂-related bloating resolves in 24–72 hours.

Return to activity

Same-day discharge for most cases. Return to office work approximately 1 week; return to heavy physical work or sport approximately 2–4 weeks.

When you'll actually feel better

Surgery: immediate pain reduction for some, longer recovery for others.
