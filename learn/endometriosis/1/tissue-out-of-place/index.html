What it actually is

Every month, the lining of your uterus (the endometrium) thickens and then sheds during your period. In endometriosis, tissue that looks and behaves like that lining ends up outside the uterus — on the ovaries, on the ligaments that hold the uterus in place, on the bladder, the bowel, the lining of the pelvis. Each month, that tissue still tries to bleed[1]Diagnosis of Endometriosis.Obstet Gynecol 2026 · PMID 41712950ACOG Clinical Practice Guideline — Diagnosis of Endometriosis. That trapped bleeding causes inflammation, scarring (called adhesions), and over time, deeply sensitized pain pathways.
