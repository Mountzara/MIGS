The osteopathic layer — the published trials

This is a DO-led CBG/MIGS practice — a fully licensed gynecologic surgeon with additional training in osteopathic manipulative treatment. For pelvic pain, OMT addresses the structural and neurological contributors that hormones and surgery don’t directly reach, layered on top of standard care rather than instead of it.

  • Muñoz-Gómez 2023 — RCT, n=41 · 8-week OMT protocol vs usual care in endometriosis-related pelvic pain. FitzGerald 2012 — the NIH multicenter pelvic-floor PT RCT

Level I evidence from 11 NIH/NIDDK research centers that a specialized internal pelvic-floor myofascial physical therapy is substantively more effective than general massage in pelvic-floor pain — which is why the “PT” you receive matters enormously.
