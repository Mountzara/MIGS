Accessibility

Mount Zara, LLC · Last reviewed August 14, 2026

Our commitment

Mount Zara is committed to a website and member portal usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technology. We build against WCAG 2.1 AA as our working standard.

What is actually done, not just intended

  • Colour contrast is measured from rendered pixels on every deployment — a release that fails contrast checks does not ship.
  • The site honours your system's reduced-motion preference: animation is suppressed for users who have asked their device for that.
  • Pages are navigable by keyboard, use semantic headings, and label their form fields.
  • Emails are plain and short, with a single clear link.

Where we fall short

Some interactive portal features are newer than our audit of them. If anything on this site is difficult or impossible for you to use, that is something we want to know and will fix — and in the meantime everything the portal does can be handled by email at info@mountzara.com, or by phone with the practice, at no disadvantage to you.

Feedback

Write to info@mountzara.com with the page address and what happened. Reports go to the people who can change the code.
